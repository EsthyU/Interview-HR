How Engineers' Online Identities Are Read in Hiring

A guided conversation about how engineering candidates' online professional identities are perceived during hiring. Each question has a short follow up to go a little deeper. There are no right or wrong answers, I am interested in your professional reasoning and lived experience.

Self completion About 12 to 15 minutes Write in your own words Fully anonymised

Workflow

Where online presence fits

How a candidate's online presence enters your process.

Q2
At what stage of your process do you check a candidate's online presence, and is this routine or situational?
Follow up
Which platforms do you actually check for engineering candidates, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolios, others, and why those particular ones?
03
Evaluation

Reading a profile

How you interpret what you find.

Q3
When you open a candidate's profile for the first time, what do you look at first to judge their credibility and suitability?
Follow up
In your experience, what separates a strong profile from a weak one?
Q4
Has information from a candidate's online presence ever changed a hiring decision you were already leaning toward?
Follow up
Tell me what happened in that specific case, what did you see, and how did it shift your thinking?
04
Trust

Credibility & status signals

How you judge what is genuine.

Q5
What makes you trust that a profile is accurate and authentic, and what patterns make you suspect exaggeration?
Follow up
Which status signals, well known employers, certifications, follower counts and the like, do you take seriously, and which do you treat with scepticism?
05
Self-presentation

Personal branding

How deliberate visibility efforts come across to you.

Q6
What personal-branding behaviours do you notice among engineering candidates, and when do they read as credible versus performative? (e.g. frequent posting, curated highlights, collecting certifications.)
Follow up
If you've hired across different markets or cultures, what differences have you noticed in what counts as a credible online presence?
06
Advice

Practical guidance

Drawing on your experience.

Q7
If you could give engineers three concrete rules for building an online presence that supports their hiring outcomes, what would they be?
Follow up
Of those three, which do you think engineers most often get wrong?
